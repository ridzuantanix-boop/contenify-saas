## Exact restrictions removed or revised

- Removed the universal instruction `two, or at most three, short clauses`.
- Removed the universal instruction `Finish every spoken word by second 11.5`.
- Removed the forced post-11.5 silent tail and replaced it with a natural completed ending before the 15-second cut.
- Replaced `one compact spoken turn` and `one concise ending` with a complete spontaneous affiliate flow.
- Replaced NX09's `Default is calm off-camera Malaysian Malay` with energetic, spontaneous off-camera Malaysian affiliate delivery.
- Removed RH17's `finish speech early` and RH19's forced quiet/minimal voice direction.
- Revised broad claim suppression into a narrower ban on objectively false concrete facts, regulated outcomes, fake commercial evidence and invented personal testimony.
- Revised generic category-only narration into a meaningful buyer-benefit direction grounded in recognizable ordinary purpose, reliable product understanding or customer-confirmed facts.
- Kept CTA finality but removed a fixed second-based deadline; the CTA now arrives near the natural ending and must complete before the cut.
- Kept Ambient and ASMR absolute: selecting a sound-only mode still removes every speech directive.

## Shared Pawarna Creative DNA

For every speaking mode, Nexabot independently creates a full Malaysian affiliate conversation:

1. Open with a product-specific audience problem, situation, discovery or question.
2. Introduce the recognizable product naturally.
3. Build one meaningful supported selling point or everyday buyer benefit.
4. Add short FOMO only when the image or customer context supplies a real signal.
5. Deliver the selected CTA conversationally near the natural ending, or complete the thought without a CTA when Tanpa CTA is selected.
6. Use fast but believable Malaysian Malay with uneven rhythm, changing emphasis, connected words, small breaths and ordinary imperfect articulation.
7. Keep factual safeguards silent; never replace an uncertain point with legal or compliance narration.

AffiMatik supplies creative direction and evidence boundaries, never spoken product sentences. Nexabot owns product interpretation, hook, dialogue, rhythm, acting and the completed video.

## Fifteen-second voice and pacing rules

- Exactly 15 seconds.
- Approximately 45–55 Malay words is an initial creative target, not an inflexible validation limit.
- Meaningful speech may run across most of the clip.
- No mandatory two-clause or three-clause maximum.
- No universal 11.5-second speech cutoff.
- No artificial speed-up and no forced long silent tail.
- CTA arrives near the natural ending and must complete before the video cut.
- After the final CTA word: no new word, syllable, phoneme, repetition, thanks, tagline, filler or corrupted speech.
- Without a CTA, the final selling thought completes naturally before the cut.

## Controls intentionally preserved

- Product Identity MAX, realistic physical scale and reference-role locks.
- Zero UI and provider watermark rules.
- Shariah/modesty requirements.
- No objectively false concrete specifications, regulated outcomes, fake prices, unsupported review/sold counts, certifications or personal testimonials.
- Customer CTA selection, Custom Creative Prompt and sound-only choices.
- No internal scriptwriter, generated-script preview or mandatory script editor.
