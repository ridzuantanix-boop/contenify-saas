## Corrected decision rule

The previous QA treated nearly every unsupported product benefit as a publication-blocking defect. That is no longer the approved creative rule.

Under the new rule, ordinary product-focused selling language, subjective enthusiasm, a selling angle and FOMO do **not** fail merely because AffiMatik did not independently verify them. Nexabot owns the creative dialogue.

The strict dialogue failure is narrower:

- reject when the presenter falsely states or implies personal use, testing, consumption or personal results;
- ordinary visible handling, possession, wearing, styling and demonstration are allowed and are not personal-use testimonials.

Product identity, CTA completion, intelligible audio, physical realism and Shariah compliance remain independent release checks. Passing the claim rule does not repair those defects.
